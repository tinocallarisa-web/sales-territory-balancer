WHAT IT IS — AND WHAT IT IS NOT

  A territory design and sales force sizing tool: how many salespeople
  each area needs, and one territory per salesperson.
  It does NOT plan routes, sequence visits or read a road network.
  Distances are straight lines times a road factor you set; travel
  time is a planning estimate.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

GETTING STARTED

  1 .... Drop the visual on the canvas
  2 .... Customer ID, Latitude, Longitude — one row per point of sale
  3 .... Visits per month and Minutes per visit — the workload
  4 .... Type a number in AREAS in the bar, or (Pro) bind your Area field
  5 .... Set HOURS / SALESPERSON / MONTH — each badge shows the salespeople needed

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

FIELD WELLS

  Customer ID ......... Any identifier — one row per point of sale
  Latitude, Longitude . Decimal degrees — required
  Visits per month .... 0.5 means every two months
  Minutes per visit ... Time on site per visit
  Postal code ......... Optional — territories made of whole postal codes
  Speed (km/h) ........ Travel speed around this point            (PRO)
  Area ................ Your commercial areas                      (PRO)
  Tooltips ............ Extra values on hover

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

THE CONTROL BAR  (works in reading view)

  Hours / salesperson . 140 — size of a territory, a hard cap
  km/h ................ 45 — average travel speed
  Road × .............. 1.3 — road distance over straight line
  Areas ............... 0 — automatic areas; 0 = nothing computed
  Export CSV .......... Client, area, territory, hours            (PRO)
  Export map PDF ...... The map as you see it, one page           (PRO)

FORMAT PANE

  TERRITORIES
    Working days / month ... 20 — hours ÷ this = one working day
    Outlier distance (km) .. 0 = off — far-off clients left out, drawn hollow

  AREA BADGES

TIPS

  ✓ Set the road factor for your country: about 1.2 United States,
    1.3 India, 1.5 Mexico, 1.6 Spain
  ✓ Check units first: minutes per visit, monthly hours
  ✓ 6.4 salespeople is the answer — rounding is your decision
  ✓ Areas follow geography, not hours; hours are balanced inside each area
  ✓ Assign by postal code? Bind Postal code: one code, one salesperson
  ✓ Click an area to see its territories and their hours, and to filter
    the rest of the page
  ✓ Try "what if 120 h" in reading view: same data, same result

TROUBLESHOOTING

  Nothing happens ............ Type a number in Areas or bind Area
  One single point ........... Bind Customer ID
  Client in the "wrong" area . Check its Area value in your data
  Postal code ignored ........ The column is not geographic
  "30,000 so far" ............ Power BI sends rows in blocks; it waits
  Export not allowed ......... Admin: "Allow downloads from custom visuals"
  Computes again on return ... Data or parameters changed, result older
                               than 29 days, not signed in, or local
                               storage switched off
