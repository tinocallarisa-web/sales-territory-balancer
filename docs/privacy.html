Privacy Policy

Sales Territory Balancer — a Power BI custom visual by TCViz · Last updated: 29 September 2026

The short version

Sales Territory Balancer does not collect, transmit, store or share any data. It has no network access of any kind. The territories are computed entirely inside the Power BI rendering sandbox on your own machine or in your own tenant, and the locations of your points of sale never leave it.

1. What the visual receives

Power BI passes the visual only the fields you place in its field wells. The visual has no route to the rest of your dataset, your Power BI workspace, your account or your device.

Field wellWhat it receives
Customer IDOne identifier per point of sale, shown in the tooltip and in the CSV export.
Latitude, LongitudeThe coordinates of each point, used in memory to compute distances and draw the map.
Hours per monthMonthly workload per point, used as its weight.
AreaOptional commercial area label; territories are built inside it. Shown in the tooltip and the CSV.
Visits per month, Minutes per visitOptional. When bound, the workload is visits × (minutes + travel time) instead of the value field.

2. Network access

The visual makes no network requests whatsoever. There are no fetch or XMLHttpRequest calls, no external scripts, no fonts loaded from a CDN, no analytics, no telemetry, no error reporting and no advertising. Its privileges array in capabilities.json is empty, which means Power BI itself blocks any outbound connection at the platform level.

This is also why the map has no street background. A background map would mean fetching tiles from a third-party server, and every tile request would tell that server which area of the world you are looking at — which, for a visual that handles customer addresses, is exactly the information that should stay inside your report. Territories read perfectly well without a basemap: what matters is their shape relative to each other.

3. What is written, and where

While a report is being edited, the visual persists the four values of its control bar — capacity in hours per month, tolerance, average speed and road factor — inside your own .pbix report file, through the standard Power BI persistProperties API, so the report opens with the settings its author chose. In reading view nothing is written: a reader can change the values to explore, and the report is not modified.

These are numbers you typed, never data values. They travel nowhere else.

What is not stored

  • No cookies, localStorage or sessionStorage
  • No browser fingerprinting or device identifiers
  • No copies of your data outside the current rendering pass
  • No personal information of any kind

4. CSV and PDF export

The Export CSV button builds a file in your browser's memory from the current assignment (customer ID, territory, workload) and hands it to your browser as a download. Export map PDF does the same with a picture of the map as it is on screen. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; the files exist only where you choose to save them.

5. Licensing

This version of the visual has no paid tier and performs no licence check. If a Pro tier is added in a future version, entitlement will be checked only through Microsoft's official IVisualLicenseManager API, which is part of Power BI itself, and this policy will be updated and dated accordingly.
