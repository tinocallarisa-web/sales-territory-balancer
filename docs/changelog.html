Changelog — Sales Territory Balancer

Current version 1.0.0.0  |  Support  |  support@tcviz.com

All notable changes are documented here. The visual follows the Power BI four-part version scheme (major.minor.patch.build).

1.0.0.0 2026-09-29

First release

  • Balanced territories computed inside Power BI. Points of sale with latitude, longitude and workload become compact territories of equal workload, travel time included. The number of territories is derived from the hours you give one salesperson rather than asked for.
  • Areas first, salespeople inside. Your own area field (province, region…) or a number of automatic geographic areas; territories are built inside each area and never cross it.
  • Parameters in a bar inside the visual — hours per salesperson, tolerance, average speed, road factor and areas — so they can be changed in reading view. In edit mode they are saved with the report; in reading view nothing is written back.
  • Two ways to give workload: visits per month plus minutes per visit, which the visual combines with travel time, or hours per month.
  • Deterministic: the same data and parameters give the same territories every time.
  • Panel with territories, points, band status, travel share and areas; one chip per area or per territory, out-of-band ones first; tooltip with customer, workload, territory and area. CSV export through Power BI's download dialog (requires the tenant setting Allow downloads from custom visuals).
  • No network requests and no background map, on purpose: the locations of your customers never leave the report. No street tiles are fetched from anyone.
