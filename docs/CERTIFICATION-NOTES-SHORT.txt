PLEASE READ FIRST - PRO PREVIEW
Without a licence, IN EDIT MODE ONLY, the Pro fields Area and Speed work under a
"Pro preview" watermark and a banner names them. Intentional, not missing gating.
In reading view, before the licence resolves, and where licences cannot be read,
the FREE result is drawn (automatic areas, bar speed). Exports (CSV, map PDF) are
Pro: without a licence they show a note and Power BI's purchase notification.

TESTING - FREE (no licence)
1. Bind Customer ID, Latitude, Longitude, Visits per month, Minutes per visit.
   The map shows grey points and asks for areas.
2. Type 5 in Areas (bar above the map): areas, badges and legend appear with the
   salespeople needed. Works in READING view too; nothing is persisted there.
3. Change Hours/salesperson, km/h or Road x: it recomputes.
4. Click a legend row or badge: other visuals filter to that area.
5. In EDIT mode bind Area or Speed: preview watermark + banner. Reading view:
   free result, no watermark.
With an active plan the same features render clean and the exports download.

EXPORTS
host.downloadService.exportVisualsContent (privilege ExportContent): CSV, and a
one-page PDF (base64) written by the visual from the map canvas. Subject to the
tenant setting "Allow downloads from custom visuals"; if blocked, a note says so.

PRIVACY / NETWORK
No fetch, XMLHttpRequest, external scripts, fonts or telemetry. Only privilege:
ExportContent. No tile basemap on purpose; outlines are embedded (Natural Earth,
src/outlines.ts). No innerHTML. Only the bar's values are persisted (edit mode).

LICENSING
Plans sales-territory-balancer-pro and partner, matched on a Service ID ending in
them; Active and Warning accepted. Requested without blocking the first paint;
Free after 5 s or on failure. isLicenseUnsupportedEnv respected. No own licence UI.

RENDERING EVENTS: one renderingStarted and one renderingFinished/Failed per
update(); balancing runs async after it; the licence repaint emits none.

Algorithm: docs/methodology.html. Reads and draws only.
Toolchain: tools 7.2.1, API 5.11.1, ESLint 9 + powerbi plugin 0 errors, audit 0.
