One salesperson has 140 hours of work, another has 60, and nobody is sure how
many people the team really needs. Redrawing sales territories usually means a
spreadsheet, a consultant or a separate mapping tool where you upload your
customer list.

Sales Territory Balancer does it inside your Power BI report. Give it your
points of sale — where they are and how much work each one takes — and it tells
you how many salespeople each area needs and proposes a territory for each one,
travel time included. Your customers' locations never leave the report.

WHAT IT DOES
• Salespeople needed per area — workload divided by one salesperson's monthly
  hours, travel included, as a figure you can round your way (6.4, not "6 or 7")
• Commercial areas — your own (province, region) or drawn automatically,
  following land borders: never across a gulf or the sea
• One territory per salesperson, compact, never above the hours you set
• Click an area to see its territories, each with its outline and its hours
• Islands and archipelagos as areas of their own
• Territories made of whole postal codes, if you assign by postal code
• Parameters in a bar inside the visual: hours per salesperson, speed, road
  factor, number of areas — editable in reading view
• Export the assignment to CSV and the map to PDF (Pro)

WHAT IT IS NOT
A territory design and sizing tool, not a routing tool. It does not plan routes
or schedule visits, and it does not read a road network: distances are straight
lines times a road factor you set, and travel time is a planning estimate.

NO NETWORK ACCESS
The visual makes no web requests and carries its own map outlines (Natural
Earth). Nothing about your customers leaves Power BI.

FREE AND PRO
Free: everything with automatic areas — sizing, territories, outlines, filters,
bookmarks, tooltips. Pro: your own Area field, speed per client, CSV and PDF
exports. While you edit without a licence, the Pro fields work under a
"Pro preview" watermark so you can see what they add.
